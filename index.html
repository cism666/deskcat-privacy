Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last Updated: 2026-04-10

繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese)

本應用程式（以下簡稱「本 App」或「懸浮喵」）尊重並保護您的個人隱私。針對本 App 的最新功能與數據處理說明如下：

  • 廣告與虛擬點數 (Google AdMob)：本 App 包含虛擬點數系統，用戶可選擇觀看影片廣告以獲得點數。我們使用 Google AdMob 作為第三方廣告夥伴，其 SDK 可能會自動收集設備識別碼（如 Advertising ID）與 IP 地址以提供廣告。我們不會手動分享任何個人資料給 AdMob。
  • 本地端圖片處理 (隱私優先)：本 App 提供 AI 2.5D 影像生成功能。我們鄭重聲明：所有圖片去背與影像運算皆完全在您的裝置本地端完成。本 App 不具備伺服器上傳功能，絕不會將您的照片、相機內容或生成後的結果上傳至任何外部雲端空間。
  • 匿名點數管理 (Firebase)：為了記錄您的點數與解鎖進度，本 App 使用匿名驗證機制。所有進度皆連結至隨機產生的匿名 ID，我們不收集您的姓名、電子郵件或電話等個人識別資訊 (PII)。請注意，若您清除 App 快取或解除安裝，匿名點數可能無法復原。
  • 權限說明：「顯示在其他應用程式上層」權限僅用於呈現電子寵物，絕不截取或記錄您的螢幕內容。
  • AI 技術宣告：視覺資產由 Wan 2.2 與 Z-image 輔助生成；3D影像技術由 Velvet Vision Lab 基於 Depth Anything V2 與 U2NetP 進行深度優化。

English (Privacy Policy Addendum)

This App (DeskCat / Suspended Meow) respects your privacy. Key points regarding our current practices:

  • Advertising and Virtual Points (Google AdMob): The app includes a point system where users earn points by watching ads. Google AdMob, our third-party partner, may automatically collect **Device IDs** (such as Advertising ID) to serve ads. We do not manually share or store personal data through this process.
  • Local-Only Image Processing: All 2.5D image generation and background removal are performed **strictly locally on your device**. This application does not possess any server-side upload capabilities for user media; your visual data remains entirely on your device and is never uploaded to any cloud servers.
  • Anonymous Point Management: We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to track your progress. No Personally Identifiable Information (PII) like names or emails is collected. **Note**: Points may be lost if local app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
  • Permissions: Overlay permission is used solely for rendering the digital pet. We do not record or capture your screen content.
  • AI Disclosure: Visuals are powered by Wan 2.2 and Z-image. 3D spatial depth is optimized using Depth Anything V2 and U2NetP by Velvet Vision Lab.
